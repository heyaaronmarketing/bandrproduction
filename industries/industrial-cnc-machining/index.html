Frequently asked questions

What's your minimum order quantity?

1 part. We regularly quote one-offs, prototypes, and low-volume production. No artificial MOQ — the economics simply favor larger quantities per setup cost.

Can you handle production runs of 100+ parts?

Yes. Once a program is proven on first article, we run production batches efficiently on the CNC platforms. Ask for a production-quantity quote alongside the prototype quote.

What industrial materials do you machine?

Full range: carbon steel (1018, 1045, 4140, 4340), stainless (304, 316, 17-4 PH), aluminum alloys, tool steels, brass, bronze, plastics (Delrin, UHMW), and exotic alloys when the application demands it.

Do you provide certificates of conformance and material MTRs?

Yes — standard on every job. Certificate of Conformance, Mill Test Report for the raw material, first-article inspection report, and dimensional verification against the print. Customer-specific quality plans and ITPs on request.

What's your typical industrial lead time?

Simple parts in stocked material: 2–5 days. Complex parts: 1–3 weeks. Production runs: 3–5 weeks. Rush service available.

Can you handle MRO / sustainment work for our production line?

Yes — reverse-engineered spares, feature-add on existing parts, and quick-turn emergency repairs for production lines that can't afford extended downtime.

Absolutely. A large share of our work is one-off replacement parts for industrial equipment — pumps, gearboxes, presses, fabrication machinery — where the OEM is out of business or lead times are unacceptable. We can reverse-engineer from a sample.

What sizes of parts can you machine?

Larger than most shops in the region. Long turned parts, big-diameter turned parts, and heavy plate/block work all fit our envelope. Call (936) 291-7827 with dimensions and we'll confirm quickly.
