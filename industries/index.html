Industries We Serve

B&R Productions is a full-service precision CNC machine shop serving four industries out of our New Waverly, Texas shop: oil & gas, aerospace, military & defense, and general industrial. Same tolerance discipline, same quality documentation, same alloy fluency across all four.

  • Oil & GasWellhead, frac pump, downhole tool, and wireline components in oilfield alloys
    The core of our shop. Inconel 718 and 625, Super Duplex 2507, 17-4 PH, 4140, and Monel K-500 for API 6A wellhead, frac pump plungers, downhole tool bodies, wireline running and pulling tools. Full material traceability, heat + lot documented.
    See industry page →
  • AerospacePrecision aerospace parts in titanium, Inconel, and aerospace aluminum alloys
    Aerospace structural brackets, engine components, and precision hardware in titanium (Ti-6Al-4V), Inconel 718, and 6061/7075 aerospace aluminum. Sub-thousandth-inch tolerance, heat/lot traceability, and CMM-verified first articles. Not AS9100-registered — best fit for prototypes, tooling, MRO, and non-controlled components.
    See industry page →
  • Military & DefenseDefense contract machining, sustainment parts, and reverse-engineered legacy hardware
    Defense-adjacent precision parts, sustainment machining for discontinued platforms, and reverse-engineered legacy hardware. Full material traceability and a US-based supply chain. Non-export-controlled work only — B&R is not ITAR-registered.
    See industry page →
  • IndustrialGeneral industrial precision parts across all common alloys
    General industrial CNC machining for equipment manufacturers, sustainment, and production runs across all common alloys. Same tolerance discipline and quality documentation as our oilfield work.
    See industry page →
  • Aerospace PartsAircraft brackets, fasteners, structural and landing-gear components — not AS9100/Nadcap certified
    Brackets, fasteners, structural components, and landing-gear hardware in Ti-6Al-4V, Inconel 718, and 17-4 PH for aerospace parts programs that don't require AS9100/Nadcap flow-down — R&D, general aviation, commercial MRO, and Tier-2 subcontractor work under a certified prime.
    See industry page →

Alloy fluency across every industry

Different industries lean on different alloys, but we run them all every week. Oil & gas: Inconel 718 and 625, Super Duplex 2507, 17-4 PH, 4140, Monel K-500. Aerospace: Titanium Ti-6Al-4V, Inconel 718, 6061 and 7075 aerospace aluminum, 15-5 PH. Defense: 17-4 PH, 4340, Inconel, and defense-spec alloys on non-export-controlled work. Industrial: 4140, 4340, 1018/1045, aluminum, tool steels, stainless — the workhorse alloys of general manufacturing.

Frequently asked questions

What CNC machining services does B&R Productions offer?

CNC turning, CNC milling, manual machining, and manual milling. Seven CNC lathes, two CNC mills, one UNISIG-class deep-hole drilling machine, plus manual lathes and mills for one-off precision work. Full-service from prototype through production.

What alloys do you machine regularly?

Inconel 718 and 625, Super Duplex 2507, 17-4 PH, 4140, 4340, Monel K-500, titanium Ti-6Al-4V, 6061 and 7075 aerospace aluminum, and 304/316 stainless. Oilfield exotic alloys weekly.

What industries do you serve?

Primarily oil & gas (frac pump, wellhead, downhole, wireline), with substantial work in aerospace, military & defense, and general industrial. Full alloy fluency across all four.

Where is B&R Productions located?

5909 Farm to Market Road 1374, New Waverly, TX 77358 — East Texas, between Huntsville and Conroe, about 60 miles north of Houston. Serving customers across Texas and the Gulf Coast.

What quality documentation do you provide?

Full material traceability, heat + lot documentation, Mill Test Reports (MTRs), first-article inspection reports, certificates of conformance, and customer-specific quality plans available on request.
